STELLAR BARRAGE

Thread the curtain. Graze the light. Break the star-gods.

▶ PLAY IN BROWSER
Free · desktop & phone · keyboard, gamepad or touch-drag · source on GitHub

Features

3 ships, 3 styles

VANTA spread nova, LANCE needle beam, SEEKER homing swarm. Each has its own speed, focus shot and screen-clearing bomb.

5 stages, 10 bosses

Every stage has a mid-boss and a multi-phase boss with named spell phases. 20 boss phases in total, ending in the Null Seraph.

Graze for score

Brush past bullets to build an x8 multiplier. Break a boss phase without bombing or dying for a PERFECT BREAK.

Power-ups & bombs

Collect power to level up your shot four times. Bombs cancel bullets into star points, and a quick bomb on hit saves you (deathbomb).

4 difficulties + practice

EASY to OVERDRIVE, a top-10 table per difficulty with name entry, and a practice mode for any stage.

Thousands of bullets

Bullets are pooled and drawn in one MultiMesh batch, so dense patterns stay smooth on phones. All art and music is generated in code.

Controls

KeyboardArrows/WASD move · Z shoot · X bomb · Shift focus (slow, shows hitbox) · Esc/P pause · M mute
TouchDrag anywhere to move (relative, your finger never covers the ship) · auto-fire while touching · BOMB and FOCUS buttons in the corners · pause top right
GamepadStick/D-pad move · A shoot · B bomb · X or shoulders focus · Start pause
